TITLE: Operation Midnight Shadow — Logistics Yard Cargo Hijack

INCIDENT OVERVIEW:
On the night of 14-June-2026 at approximately 22:30 hours, an armed contingent intercepted and hijacked a high-value transit container containing imported electronics components and computing hardware at the Inland Container Depot (ICD), Tughlakabad logistics corridor.

PRIMARY SUSPECT INVOLVEMENT:
1. CCTV footage analysis from Gate #3 and yard surveillance cameras identifies suspect Aarav Verma (PERSON_017, alias "Shadow") coordinating the unmanifested cargo offloading.
2. Inside yard accomplice Rohan Gupta (PERSON_005) deliberately manipulated power breakers to disable perimeter spotlights between 22:15 and 22:45.
3. Logistics transport vehicle Bolero Pickup (Registration: DL-01-AB-1234 / VEHICLE_017) was captured exiting the depot toward Badarpur border at high speed.

RECOVERED LEADS:
Digital triage indicates suspect Aarav Verma utilized an untraceable burner line for operational dispatch instructions.
Investigation transferred to multi-jurisdiction intelligence desk for cross-state receiver tracing.
